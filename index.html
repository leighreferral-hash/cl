Core Discipline

⚠️ Non-Licensed Scope CSC facilitates process, documents facts, and routes. Licensed staff and carriers make coverage, settlement, premium-impact, reinstatement eligibility, and legal determinations. Never predict outcomes, estimate payouts, confirm coverage after a lapse, or advise on strategy.

Always / Never

✅ ALWAYS
  • Verify account + caller authorization first
  • Document verified facts, triggers, and routing
  • Acknowledge concern with empathy
  • Use approved transfer language
  • Route same day when escalation is required
  • Give carrier 24/7 number for active losses after hours
  • Check status per policy
❌ NEVER
  • Interpret coverage, exclusions, or claim merit
  • Promise outcomes, settlements, or reinstatement
  • Discuss premium impact or strategy
  • Confirm coverage or certificate validity after a lapse
  • Quote refund or return premium amounts
  • Collect or advise on Statement of No Loss
  • Leave claim details in voicemail

Standard Call Flow

  1. Greeting — “Thank you for calling Acrisure, this is [Your Name]. How can I help you today?”
  2. Account Lookup — Locate client in Epic by name, code, or identifier.
  3. Authorization — Verify business name, mailing address, caller name & role.
  4. Identify Request — Payment, reinstatement, cancellation, claim, certificate, etc.
  5. Answer or Route — Facts only. Stop at licensed determinations.
  6. Document — Create activity, associate to policy, structured notes.
  7. Close — “Is there anything else I can do for you? Thank you for being an Acrisure client.”

High-Stakes Reminders

A commercial lapse can:

  • Breach contract requirements
  • Void outstanding certificates
  • Trigger lender force-placed insurance
  • Add to existing audit balances
  • Create claims-made prior-acts gaps

Reinstatement truths:

  • Never guaranteed — carrier decides
  • Payment alone is not enough
  • May reinstate with or without a gap
  • Statement of No Loss is licensed-only
  • Past the window → new application path

Standard Call Workflow

Every inbound call follows this structure. Consistency protects the client and reduces E&O risk.

  1. Greeting
    “Thank you for calling Acrisure, this is [Your Name]. How can I help you today?”
  2. Account Lookup in Epic
    Search by business name, client code, or unique account identifier.
  3. Authorization Verification
    “Can I get the business name and the mailing address on the policy?”
    “And who am I speaking with, and your role with [Business Name]?”
    🔒 Note Never discuss balances, policy details, or claim information until authorization is confirmed.
  4. Identify the Request Type
    Payment / Billing · Reinstatement · Cancellation · Claim / FNOL · Certificate · Audit · General Inquiry
  5. Answer Factually or Route
    Provide only verified facts. At any licensed determination → stop and route (warm transfer preferred).
  6. Document in Epic
    Create the correct activity (CBIQ, CCRF, CCN1, etc.), associate to the policy, structured notes.
  7. Close the Call
    “Is there anything else I can do for you? Thank you for being an Acrisure client.”

Payments & Billing Inquiries

Time-sensitive. A commercial lapse can breach contracts, void certificates, and trigger force-placed coverage.

What CSC Can Do Allowed

  • Verify account and authorization
  • Provide factual current balance, due date, last payment, payment history
  • Separate current premium vs audit balance when visible
  • Facilitate payment path (carrier transfer, portal, agency e-Pay link)
  • State factual deadlines on grace / pending cancellation
  • Document everything and set accurate expectations

Stop Points Do Not Cross

  • Do not process payment yourself unless the process specifically allows it
  • Do not guarantee that payment will keep coverage active or prevent cancellation
  • Do not promise reinstatement or coverage continuity
  • Do not resolve audit balance disputes
  • Do not confirm certificate validity after any lapse

Approved Spiels

Current balance / due date

“I can see the amount currently showing as due is [amount], with a due date of [date]. For payment support, I can transfer you to the carrier billing department.”

Grace period

“I see the payment is past due, and the policy has not yet been cancelled. The deadline showing is [date]. I can transfer you to the carrier billing department for payment assistance.”

Pending cancellation

“I see a pending cancellation date of [date]. I cannot guarantee the outcome, but I can transfer you to the carrier billing department so payment options can be reviewed directly.”

Agency Bill

“I can send you an e-Pay link so you can complete the payment securely. What email should I use?”

Direct Bill

“This policy is set up for Direct Bill, which means payment is made directly to the insurance carrier. Our office cannot take that payment on the carrier’s behalf.”

Avoid over-promising

“I don’t want to guarantee something that requires carrier approval.”

Key Facts

Direct Bill

Warm transfer to Carrier billing / portal.

Agency Bill

Send e-Pay link or route to P&C Servicer 1.

Current Premium

Ongoing policy billing installments.

Audit Balance

Prior-period adjustment. Keep separate from current premium.

Documentation (CBIQ)

  • Caller name + role + authorization status
  • Reason for call · Current policy status
  • Amounts / dates discussed · Information provided
  • Transfer / route destination · Expectation language used

Reinstatement Requests

Highest-stakes payment-related call. Reinstatement is never guaranteed — it is carrier-determined.

🚨 Critical Payment does not equal reinstatement. Even if reinstated, there may be a lapse period with no coverage. Statement of No Loss is a licensed/carrier matter only.

What CSC Can Do

  • Verify status and cancellation date
  • Gather the reinstatement request
  • Explain that reinstatement is carrier-determined
  • Document thoroughly · Route to carrier representative or licensed agent

Stop Points Absolute

  • Do not say the policy is eligible, will be approved, or is likely to be reinstated
  • Do not collect, explain, validate, or advise on Statement of No Loss
  • Do not discuss coverage for any loss that occurred during the lapse
  • Do not confirm certificate or prior-acts protection after a lapse
  • If past the reinstatement window → route for new application discussion

Approved Spiels

“Because the policy shows cancelled as of [date], this becomes a reinstatement-related question. I will document what you shared and route you to the appropriate licensed or carrier representative.”
“I understand you’d like to make the payment right away. Because the policy is showing as cancelled, making a payment does not automatically mean coverage has been restored.”
“I don’t want to give you incorrect information. Payment alone does not allow me to guarantee that the policy will be reinstated. The carrier or authorized policy team must confirm whether reinstatement is approved and the effective date.”
“That is tied to a reinstatement review, so I am not able to advise on or collect that. I will document your request and route it to the appropriate licensed path.”
“I understand the concern. I cannot confirm coverage or certificate validity after a lapse, but I will document the details and route this to the appropriate team.”
“Unfortunately this is beyond the carrier’s window, so this policy can’t be brought back through reinstatement. What I can do is have an agent discuss new coverage options so the business is protected going forward.”

Commercial Fallout to Flag in Notes

  • Outstanding certificates & additional insureds may have been invalid during the lapse
  • Lienholder / mortgagee may have triggered force-placed coverage
  • Claims-made policies: possible gap in retroactive / prior-acts coverage
  • Contract requirements for continuous coverage may have been breached
📌 Process Pattern Verify → State facts → Stop → Document → Route

Cancellations

One of the most sensitive call types. Lead with empathy. Never confirm coverage status, gaps, or refund amounts.

⚡ Note Cancellation can create: coverage gaps, breach of contract, uninsured claim exposure, invalidated certificates, lender force-placed insurance, and remaining audit balances.

Two Main Types

TypeOriginCSC Action
Carrier-Driven (NOC)Insured received Notice of CancellationWarm transfer to licensed team. Never confirm or deny coverage.
Insured-DrivenClient requests cancellationDocument request, ask about replacement coverage, flag retention, route to licensed team.

Key Question

“Do you already have replacement coverage bound?”

Purpose: help prevent lapse, protect the business, avoid contract violations, reduce uninsured exposure.

Approved Spiels

“I understand why you are asking. I cannot confirm coverage status, but I can document the concern and connect you with the licensed team who can review it accurately.”
“I understand the urgency. Coverage gap questions require licensed review. I am going to route this as a priority and include the effective date and your callback information in the note.”
“Refund amounts and return premium calculations need to be reviewed through the appropriate process. I can document your refund question and route it for follow-up.”
“I can document that you are requesting cancellation and that replacement coverage may be involved. The licensed team needs to review the cancellation terms and effective date before this is finalized.”
“I want to make sure you receive accurate information. These cancellation questions can affect coverage, refund, or policy terms, so they need to be reviewed by the licensed team.”

Common Reasons (Insured-Driven)

New carrier selected Business sold / closed Asset sold Operations changed Premium concerns Service dissatisfaction Contract ended

Claims / FNOL / Status

Facilitate reporting and provide factual status only. Adjusters and licensed staff determine coverage and outcomes.

Three-Part Decision Model

  1. Can CSC answer factually?
    Verified facts only: claim status on record, carrier contact, documents received, callback details.
  2. Where is the stop point?
    Stop at interpretation, advice, outcome prediction, claim strategy, attorney handling, large liability, total loss, demand, premium impact, or disputed settlement.
  3. What is the routing path?
    Licensed team, Claims team, AM, carrier claims contact, or back-office. Document the trigger and route same day when needed.

FNOL – Capture Facts Only

  • Date of loss · Time of loss · Location
  • Type / line of business
  • Factual description (caller’s words — no fault, liability, or causation language)
  • Parties / vehicles / injured persons
  • Injuries (yes/no) and treatment · Witnesses / police report numbers
  • Current condition of property or vehicle
📌 Routing Auto / Property / GL → *CCLP workgroup. Workers’ Comp / Professional-Management Liability / Other → P&C Servicer 1.

Approved Spiels

“I can confirm the factual claim status we have on file. The question about whether that means coverage applies is a licensed coverage question, so I am going to connect you with the appropriate licensed team member.”
“That is a licensed coverage question, so I am going to connect you with the appropriate licensed team member.”
“The carrier will review the claim and determine next steps. I can document what you shared and route this to the appropriate team for review.”
“Premium impact questions need to be reviewed by the licensed Acrisure team.”
“First — is everyone safe, and is anyone hurt? If this is an emergency or someone’s injured, make sure 911 has been called before we do anything else.”
“Because this is happening right now, the fastest path is the carrier’s 24/7 claims line at [number] — they can assign an adjuster immediately.”
“The fastest way to get an adjuster assigned is the carrier’s 24/7 claims line at [number], or their portal. I can also take the first notice of loss and submit it for you.”
“I will document that a demand letter has been received and route this to the appropriate internal claims path. I cannot advise on legal handling.”

Urgent Escalation Triggers

Attorney / demand / lawsuit Fatality Loss ≥ $100,000 Significant reserve increase Litigation filed Workers’ Comp Professional / Mgmt Liability Cyber / data breach Employment practices

Audit Questions & Disputes

An audit is a carrier review to confirm the information used to calculate the premium was accurate.

What CSC Can Do

  • Explain the general audit process at a high level
  • Separate factual current premium vs audit balance when visible
  • Document the caller’s concern · Route disputes for review

Stop Points

  • Do not recalculate or adjudicate the audit
  • Do not agree that figures are wrong or promise a correction
  • Do not advise on strategy for disputing the audit

Approved Spiels

“I can document that you are questioning the audit balance. Audit disputes must be reviewed through the appropriate account or licensed path, so I will route this for follow-up.”
“I understand you’re questioning the amount. I can document the concern and direct it to the team that handles audit reviews. I’m not able to change or determine the audit result on this call.”
“I can explain the process, but disputes require review with the carrier.”

Certificates & Document Requests

Provide permitted documents when authorized. Never interpret wording or confirm post-lapse validity.

What CSC Can Send (when authorized)

Policies Endorsements Audit documents Cancellation notices Reinstatement notices Certificates of Insurance Auto ID cards Evidence of Insurance

Certificate requests: Certificates_CL@acrisure.com

Stop Points

  • Do not interpret policy language or certificate wording
  • Do not confirm that a certificate remains valid after a lapse
  • Do not share account details with unauthorized third parties

Approved Spiels

“I can only share account details with an authorized contact.”
“I can send the document; interpretation should go to an Account Manager.”
“I understand the concern. I cannot confirm coverage or certificate validity after a lapse, but I will document the details and route this to the appropriate team.”
“I can take the certificate request details and check the information on file. I’ll follow our certificate process and have any special wording or coverage questions reviewed by the appropriate team.”

General Service & Multi-Topic Calls

Factual policy information, renewal status, and multi-topic requests.

What CSC Can Provide (Factual Only)

  • Current balance, due date, payment history
  • Active policies, carrier names
  • Effective dates, expiration dates, renewal dates
  • Documents on file (and send permitted ones)

Phrases to Avoid

These create E&O exposure. Use the approved alternative every time.

Avoid Saying Use Instead
“This should be covered.” / “I think you’ll be covered.” “Coverage depends on the policy terms, conditions, and exclusions. The Claims Team or a licensed representative will need to review the details.”
“You should file the claim.” “I can document the information available now and connect you with the right team or carrier contact for next steps.”
“The carrier will pay this.” / “The carrier will pay.” “The carrier will review the claim and make a decision under the policy.”
“This will not impact your premium.” / “Claims usually raise premiums.” “Whether a claim affects your premium depends on several factors, including the type of claim, policy, and carrier rating guidelines.”
“You can reopen the claim.” “Questions about reopening or disputing a claim need to be reviewed by the licensed Acrisure team.”
“Don’t worry, this will be handled.” / “We’ll get this fixed today.” “I’m sorry to hear that happened. I’ll make sure the information is documented clearly and routed to the right team. I can’t guarantee a completion time.”
“You’re covered.” “I can confirm the policy information shown in our system, but I cannot determine whether this particular loss is covered.”
“Your payment went through.” (without confirmation) “The system currently shows [pending/submitted/posted]. I can only confirm payment once the approved system reflects it.”
“Your policy is reinstated.” (without official confirmation) “The carrier or authorized policy team must confirm whether reinstatement is approved and the effective date.”
Any guarantee of reinstatement or coverage after a lapse “Reinstatement is carrier-determined. I will document and route this for review. Please don’t assume coverage is active until you receive confirmation.”
Confirming coverage status or certificate validity after a lapse “I cannot confirm coverage or certificate validity after a lapse. I will document the details and route this to the appropriate team.”
“That’s not a fair settlement.” “I understand you feel the settlement offer does not reflect your expectations. I can document your concerns and route them to the assigned adjuster for review.”
Promising a specific callback time or outcome “I’ve documented the concern and routed it to the appropriate team. I’m unable to guarantee a specific response time.”
💡 Golden rule Be empathetic, factual, neutral, and clear. Do not guess, interpret coverage, decide liability, negotiate settlements, authorize repairs, guarantee payment, or promise reinstatement unless your role and approved procedures expressly authorize that action.

Documentation Standards

Every call must leave a clear, complete record for the next person and for audit readiness.

Universal Note Elements

  • Caller name + contact information
  • Authorization status / relationship
  • Exact question or concern (use trigger language when possible)
  • Policy number(s) involved
  • Factual information provided · Actions taken
  • Routing / transfer destination
  • Follow-up expectation / callback commitment
  • Dates mentioned and urgency level · Documents referenced or sent
